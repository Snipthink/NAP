Operating Principle

We join the team.

NAP does not operate from the sidelines. Depending on the mandate, we can combine strategy, operational participation, network activation, industrial relationships and capital to help build the next stage.

Complex systems fail at the interfaces — between strategy and operations, between capital and regulation, between a good idea and the people who need to trust it. That is where we work.

Specific participation, investment and operating roles depend on the mandate and are agreed individually.
