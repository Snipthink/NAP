The Institution

Built on decades of execution.

Norwegian Aviation Partners was formalized around one conviction: strategy without operating experience is a guess. The institution exists to close the gap between what should happen and what actually gets built.

Origin

Two builders, one studio.

Norwegian Aviation Partners was founded by Terje Kleppan and Petter Kleppan — a father and son whose careers ran in parallel through different industries before converging into a single operating model.

Terje built his career inside commercial aviation: procurement, MRO, aircraft transformation and the relationships that keep fleets flying. Petter moved through deep technology, space systems, industrial manufacturing and venture building. NAP formalizes what they had already been doing informally for years — combining relational trust with systems thinking to help complex ventures actually get built.

Aviation Legacy

Forty years on the ground.

Terje's aviation career spans aircraft procurement, cabin transformation, MRO network development and airline infrastructure — built through direct, hands-on involvement rather than advisory distance.

That experience shapes how NAP approaches every mandate: aircraft do not scale on PowerPoint, and trust in aviation is built through delivered work, not decks.

Deep-Tech Evolution

From industrial systems to space.

Petter's path moved through industrial manufacturing, battery technology, data and drone operations, and space-adjacent programs — building fluency in the systems, capital structures and regulatory environments that deep-tech ventures require.

That evolution is what allows NAP to operate credibly at the intersection of aviation and the next industrial frontier: advanced materials, orbital logistics and space infrastructure.

The NAP Model

Why we formalized the studio.

Conventional advisory models stop at the recommendation. NAP was built because the operators, founders and institutions we work with needed a partner willing to stay through structuring, relationship activation and execution — not hand over a report and leave.

We know what happens after the strategy deck. Networks are built through trust. We work where strategy meets reality.

Philosophy

Mobility, technology and ecology are one system.

Technology should expand human possibility without disconnecting us from the systems that sustain life. NAP believes next-generation aviation, advanced materials, sustainable energy and ecological industries can evolve together.
