Founding Partner

Terje Kleppan

Infrastructure & Aviation Operations

Terje's aviation career spans four decades, built directly inside procurement, cabin transformation, MRO networks and airline infrastructure.

His work has covered aircraft procurement and acquisition, aircraft interior transformation programs, the development of maintenance and repair networks, airline development, and the infrastructure and global relationships required to keep complex fleets operating. Experience across this career spans major aircraft acquisition and asset programs, dozens of MRO network relationships, and a substantial volume of aircraft transformation projects.

Founding Partner

Petter Kleppan

Systems & Industrial Architecture

Petter's career moved through deep technology, industrial manufacturing and space-adjacent systems — building the systems and capital fluency the next industrial frontier requires.

His experience includes involvement with a NASA Commercial Orbital Transportation Services (COTS) related program associated with Rocketplane Kistler, engagement with early-stage space ventures including Boriken Space and Obitec, industrial technology and data operations through Grupo Senseta, and an industrial manufacturing and battery technology program associated with Lithium Werks, including an associated factory support package. His broader background spans industrial manufacturing, battery technology, deep-tech venture building, data analytics, space systems and new mobility.
