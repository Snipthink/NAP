Nils Henrik Sanner

Global Board of Advisors

Nils Henrik Sanner brings extensive commercial aviation experience to NAP's advisory board, with a career spanning pilot operations, flight safety and large-scale workforce management within the Norwegian aviation ecosystem.

His experience includes operating in extreme-weather conditions and managing the operational demands unique to Norwegian and Nordic aviation — a perspective that informs NAP's approach to infrastructure, safety and regional connectivity.
